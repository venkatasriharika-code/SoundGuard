# SoundGuard demo audio provenance

SoundGuard's demonstration audio will use selected **normal** and **anomalous** industrial-machine recordings from the MIMII/DCASE ecosystem. The original MIMII dataset is published by Hitachi, Ltd. on Zenodo under **CC BY-SA 4.0** and contains normal and anomalous recordings from pumps, fans, valves, and slide rails, including realistic background-factory noise. Source: https://zenodo.org/records/3384388

For the interactive demo, selected clips will be drawn from the public DCASE 2023 Task 2 distribution mirrored as `renumics/dcase23-task2-enriched` on Hugging Face. Its dataset card describes 10-second normal and anomalous machine recordings associated with MIMII DG and ToyADMOS2 and notes the original distribution's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/renumics/dcase23-task2-enriched

The website must label these recordings as **benchmark demo audio** and may not present them as SoundGuard's own factory-pilot recordings. Any project presentation should cite the original dataset source and license.
